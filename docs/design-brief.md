# Save Sixes Rd redesign brief

## Objective

Create a modern, trustworthy community campaign website that makes the case against overdevelopment on Sixes Road easy to understand and makes the next action obvious.

## Preserve

- Existing public content and campaign evidence
- Recognizable Save Sixes identity and logo assets where useful
- Primary routes: `/`, `/blog`, `/library`, `/about`, and `/petiton`
- Petition and newsletter conversion paths
- Existing public document links unless replaced with locally hosted copies

## Improve

- Visual hierarchy and credibility
- Campaign storytelling on the homepage
- Mobile navigation and reading experience
- Presentation of documents, plans, and analysis
- Newsletter and petition calls to action
- Admin workflow for future updates

## Constraints

- Public content may be reused as source material
- Private subscribers and Squarespace account data require an authenticated export
- Do not change legal, consent, or petition wording without explicit review
- The initial site should be deployable on Railway
